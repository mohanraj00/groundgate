2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The maintenance dose of roflumilast tablet is one 500 micrograms (mcg) tablet per day, with or without food.
Starting treatment with a dose of Roflumilast tablets 250 mcg once daily for 4 weeks and increasing to Roflumilast tablets 500 mcg once daily thereafter may reduce the rate of treatment discontinuation in some patients [see Clinical Studies (14.1)]. However, 250 mcg per day is not the effective (therapeutic) dose.

The maintenance dose for patients with COPD is one 500 mcg tablet per day, with or without food. Starting treatment with a dose of roflumilast tablets 250 mcg once daily for 4 weeks and increasing to roflumilast tablets 500 mcg once daily thereafter may reduce the rate of treatment discontinuation in some patients. 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Roflumilast 250 mcg tablets supplied as yellowish, round, beveled-edge tablets, debossed with "A" over "116" on one side and plain on other side.
Roflumilast 500 mcg tablets are supplied as white to off-white, round, beveled-edge tablets, debossed with "A" over "117" on one side and plain on other side.
