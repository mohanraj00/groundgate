Capital Gains or Losses Sale of Main Home (cont'd)

Sale of a home received through inheritance or as a gift is Out of Scope unless it has been used as a
personal residence by the taxpayer or spouse. The taxpayer must provide the cost basis of the
residence. Tests 1 and 2 then apply to exclude the gain.

Death of spouse. If the taxpayer sells their home within 2 years after their spouse dies and has not remar-
ried as of the sale date, they can count any time their spouse owned the home as time they owned it and
any time when the home was their spouse’s residence as time when it was their residence. In addition, the
taxpayer may be able to increase their exclusion amount from $250,000 to $500,000 if the taxpayer or their
deceased spouse meet the requirements for Test 1 and both the taxpayer and their deceased spouse meet
the requirement for Test 2.
