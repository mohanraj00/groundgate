# Benchmark set 2: spec 0.1 and 0.2 side by side

> **Not final.** 10 of the 10 control items are not judged yet, so the controls section is incomplete.

Generated by `bench/score.py --set bench/set2` from the cached runs in `runs/`. [README.md](README.md) explains the set and [SELECTION.md](SELECTION.md) how it was picked. Gold: 74 documents checked in full, 400 facts, 96 fields confirmed absent, 4 excluded as ambiguous (checked by a person in 2.4 hours). 27 control documents are judged only where the two specs decide differently: 10 candidates.
