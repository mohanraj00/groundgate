# Benchmark set 2: spec 0.1 and 0.2 side by side

Generated by `bench/score.py --set bench/set2` from the cached runs in `runs/`. [README.md](README.md) explains the set and [SELECTION.md](SELECTION.md) how it was picked. Every candidate is decided twice: by the spec 0.2 core in this repository, and by the released 0.1.0 wheel through `bench/decide.py`. Both see the same candidates.

Gold: 74 documents checked in full, 401 facts, 93 fields confirmed absent, 6 excluded as ambiguous (checked by a person in 2.5 hours). 27 control documents are judged only where the two specs decide differently: 10 candidates. Three fields changed after the first scoring, with model output in view; README.md lists them under "Gold changes after scoring".
