Is this text the label's recommended dosage for the population shown? Check the population (adults or pediatric patients) and the dosage text: both must be right.
The left side is the label text, sections 2 and 3. The orange box is the text that the value cites, and the yellow mark is the value in it. A value is right only when the label gives it as a recommended dosage for that population. A dosage for the other population, or a text that is not a dosage, is wrong. A part of a dosage is right when it states a dosage for the population by itself. Use Not sure when you can't tell.
